About

Software engineer by trade. Artist at night.

That's what I like to say if I was a model citizen of Earth, but really I don't put much weight into career aspirations. What your true wishes in life is what determines your character.

—What are my aspirations? To pursue my animations, my writings, my art.

I used to post a certain webnovel over on a royal website from around Spring of 2020 to Spring of 2023. It was an adaptation of a story I strung together since the golden years of my anime watching. Indeed, I got pretty burnt out of it and found it difficult to move forward with the story, thus I put it on endless hiatus with a slim hope of continuation... or just a full reboot. I know only of one person that knows what that webnovel is, hello if your reading!

During the world shutdown that was 2020, specifically December of that year and right when I was entering my Winter break of that semester, the wave of V-tubers struck me like a wave of bliss. I was utterly entranched by their care-free attitudes towards the world that I had to get involved somehow. Thus, I chose all the subject matter that enraptured me at the time and went with it. There came Mumei: The Dawn of Humanity, in current development!

Some of my favorite anime / manga / light novels of all time definitely informs my world view and approach to life. A small subset (very small!) of that:

  • Hunter x Hunter
  • Chainsaw Man
  • 86
  • Parasyte: The Maxim
  • Puella Magi Madoka Magica
  • Classroom of the Elite
